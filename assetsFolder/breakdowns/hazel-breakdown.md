## About the project

Hazel is currently in production.

Theme park walkaround characters are a particular fixation of mine. Not the ones in suits like Mickey or Minnie, but the face characters- the ones who you can actually talk to without having to play charades to figure out what they're trying to say. My current favorite is Ygor over in Dark Universe at Universal Studios Epic Universe; who inspired me to develop my own. So in contemporary theme park fashion, I always start with some IP...

The concept of Critical Role Land, a theme park based on the characters and events of its namesake actual-play D&D series, is a long-running joke in the Critical Role community. Since the inception of this 100% totally-not-real theme park, Critical Role as intellectual property as swelled in capital and popularity, which the makes Hazel marginally more feasible.

## Lore

Hazel Finch (she/her) hails from the farming town of Gelvaan, and is currently an artificing student at Dayal Hall. Born and raised in an agricultural community, her affinity for animals often appears in her automaton work. She wears her heart on her sleeve, and is often in awe of the bustling spires of Jrusar. Hazel is a low-level artificer with a homuculus servant bird automaton, and actors portraying Hazel should be comfortable working with a simple animatronic.

## Interface

The most mentally-cumbersome part of this concept is the animatronic interface, so I'll phrase this section of the project as a research objective: 

Determine how a small, wearable interface can enable a live performer to reliably command a semi-autonomous animatronic character without diverting attention from performance, guests, navigation, and safety. This work will investigate the interface as a form of shared human-robot control. In this model, autonomy handles routine idle movement, while the performer retains authority over socally-meaningful actions and safety-critical interruptions.

The interface has a target completion date of December 2026.
